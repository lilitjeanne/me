Professional bio

Lilit Melikyan is a Senior Project Manager with 14 years of experience spanning telecommunications, cybersecurity, and direct carrier billing. She has built her career at the intersection of complex technical integration and client-facing excellence — guiding enterprise clients through onboarding, managing strategic partnerships, building scalable operational frameworks, and delivering measurable outcomes across fast-moving international environments.

From her early years as a Billing and CRM engineer at Orange Armenia to leading cross-border partnerships at Orange International Carriers and managing cybersecurity integration projects at Evina, Lilit has consistently grown at the intersection of technology and business. Her most recent role at Cryptonext Security saw her overseeing Post-Quantum Cryptographic pilot projects with enterprise clients across the banking, financial infrastructure, and energy sectors.

She holds an International MBA from IESEG School of Management in Paris and is a PMP-certified professional with a Master's in Management of Information Systems. Known for her ability to operate across cultures and time zones — having served clients across Latin America, Europe, the UK, and the Middle East — Lilit brings a rare blend of strategic vision and hands-on execution to every engagement.
